FATE'S EDGE PRESS KIT

This folder contains material for reviewers, streamers, convention organizers,
game stores, podcasters, and anyone trying to explain Fate's Edge without
reverse-engineering a repository.

CONTENTS

fates-edge-fact-sheet.html
    A printable overview with verified project facts, links, and contact details.

fates-edge-press-copy.txt
    One-line, short, medium, and long descriptions ready to quote or adapt.

fates-edge-flyer.html
    A self-contained, single-page handout for print or a table display.

demo.mp4
    A short silent tour of the browser toolkit.

demo-thumbnail.png
    The video cover image.

toolkit-home.png, toolkit-encounter.png, toolkit-tour.png
    Interface images suitable for editorial coverage with attribution.
